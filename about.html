My story

How I got here

My journey into technology started with simple HTML websites and a willingness to understand what was happening behind the screen. That curiosity grew into a deeper interest in software engineering, system architecture, cybersecurity, and the infrastructure that makes modern digital products possible.

I quickly realized that software is not just about writing code — it is about understanding systems, trade-offs, reliability, and constraints. The more I learned, the more I appreciated the difference between a demo that looks impressive and a product that still works when users, data, and time pressure show up.

Today, I work across the software development lifecycle, from designing system architectures and building APIs to developing web applications, automating workflows, and integrating intelligent technologies. I’m particularly interested in building systems that are scalable, maintainable, secure, and practical beyond the development environment.

I care deeply about the connection between engineering quality and product value. I want the systems I build to not only function, but also to be trustworthy, understandable, and useful in real-world conditions.
